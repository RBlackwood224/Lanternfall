# Lanternfall

**Hunt of the Wisp** — a dark pixel-art survival game for the browser.

Every full moon a creature haunts the village. The villagers carry lanterns through the fog, search the houses for oil and light the old altars to force the creature into flesh — then banish it with silver daggers, or simply survive until dawn. The creature hunts them in the dark, blows out their lanterns, mimics their cries for help and drags them to the cage in the crypt.

- Play as a villager (Priest, Blacksmith, Herb-woman, Night watchman, Miller's lad) or as the creature (Wisp, Witch, Werewolf)
- Solo against the computer, couch co-op for two, or online with up to 6 friends
- Keyboard, controller and touch screen
- English, French and Hungarian — every player chooses their own language, even in the same online game
- Special nights: thunderstorm, thick fog, blood moon

Play it here: https://rblackwood224.github.io/Lanternfall/
